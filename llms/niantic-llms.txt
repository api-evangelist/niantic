# Niantic Spatial

> Niantic Spatial, Inc. builds real-world foundation models for physical AI. Our Large Geospatial Model (LGM) — trained on 30+ billion posed real-world images — enables people, robots, and AI systems to understand and operate in physical environments through geometrically accurate 3D reconstruction, precise localization, and spatial understanding. Core enterprise focus areas are robotics, defense, and oil and gas.

## Platform

Niantic Spatial provides a three-part geospatial AI platform — Reconstruct, Localize, and Understand — powered by the Large Geospatial Model:

- **Reconstruct**: Fuses multi-sensor inputs into accurate 3D models (Gaussian splats and textured meshes) for simulation, inspection, and planning. https://www.nianticspatial.com/products/reconstruct
- **Localize (VPS)**: Industry-leading, centimeter-level, 6DoF visual positioning that works indoors, outdoors, and in GPS-denied environments. No prior scanning required at global scale; near-centimeter accuracy in Scaniverse-mapped locations. Used by tens of millions of users. Supports robots, AI agents, AR devices, and mobile applications. https://www.nianticspatial.com/products/localize
- **Understand**: Semantic understanding at every 3D point in a geo-referenced map, enabling LLMs and AI agents to query the physical world in natural language. https://www.nianticspatial.com/products/understand
- **Capture**: Tools and services to acquire high-quality 3D aerial and ground data, including the Scaniverse app and on-demand mapping. https://www.nianticspatial.com/products/capture
- **Scaniverse**: Enterprise-grade mobile scanning app for iOS and Android. Integrated web and mobile platform for data capture, spatial asset generation, mesh and splat inspection, and VPS localization. https://scaniverse.nianticspatial.com/
- **Niantic Spatial Development Kit (NSDK) 4.0**: Unified SDK for Unity, Swift, Android, and ROS 2, connecting directly to Scaniverse and VPS 2.0.

## Large Geospatial Model (LGM)

The LGM is Niantic Spatial's foundational AI model for physical world understanding:

- Trained on 30+ billion posed real-world images from ground and overhead sensors
- Adds spatial reasoning capabilities to Large Language Models (LLMs)
- Delivers per-pixel semantic analysis and tracks 200+ object types
- Enables queryable 3D maps — users and machines can ask open-ended spatial questions
- Differentiator: Unlike general-purpose models, the LGM is grounded in precise real-world geometry and coordinates, not simulation or synthetic data
- More: https://www.nianticspatial.com/blog/largegeospatialmodel
